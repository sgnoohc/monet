One piece's F, Li and τi are tiny, so their arrows are drawn as if the piece held the whole mass (× the number of pieces). v is drawn at true scale ×0.5.

From one piece to I ω

Every piece moves in a circle about the axis, so its speed is set by its distance from the axis:

vi = ω × ri,   |vi| = ω r⊥i

Its angular momentum about O has a z part that only depends on r⊥:

Li,z = (ri × dmi vi)z = dmi r⊥i² ω

Every piece shares the same ω, so it factors out of the sum:

Lz = Σ dmi r⊥i² ω = I ω,   I = Σ dmi r⊥i² → ∫ r⊥² dm

Switch Pieces to Fine and the sum moves toward the continuum formula. Move the axis off the center of mass and I grows by Md², the parallel-axis theorem.

With a torque applied, each piece also gets a tangential acceleration α r⊥, and

Σ τi,z = Σ dmi r⊥i² α = I α = dLz/dt

The forces between pieces cancel in pairs, as in the N-body page, so only the external torque changes L. For the thick block, a piece above or below the plane of O has an Li that tilts away from the axis. A matching piece on the other side tilts the other way, so the sum stays along ω.
