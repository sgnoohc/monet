Physics simulations

Interactive demonstrations for PHY2060. Each one runs in the browser with nothing to install.

N-body angular momentum

1–15 bodies under mutual gravity. Total momentum P and angular momentum L about any point stay constant with no external force.

Rigid body rotation

Build up the moment of inertia I = Σ dm r⊥² piece by piece, and see L = Iω and τ = Iα.

Transverse and longitudinal waves

The same s(x, t) drawn on a string and in a column of particles. Track one particle and compare its motion with the wave's.
