Notes

Transverse: particles move perpendicular to the wave's travel. A string, light, S waves.

Longitudinal: particles move along the wave's travel, bunching into compressions (C) and spreading into rarefactions (R). Sound, a Slinky, P waves.

v = λ/T = fλ

Both pictures use the same displacement s(x, t). The graph shows it; turn the longitudinal displacement 90° and you get the transverse shape.

A compression is not at a crest of the graph. It sits where s crosses zero going down: the particle to its left has moved right and the one to its right has moved left.
