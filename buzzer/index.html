    A neg is a buzz before the reading finishes, shown as a negative time (how early you were). With an early buffer set, a buzz that close before the end counts as on time instead, and its bar dips below zero in green. In Voice mode that early time is estimated from the reading pace. In Voice mode the clock starts when your browser reports the speech has finished, which can trail the last syllable by about 50–150 ms. Text-only mode starts the clock the moment the last word appears.
